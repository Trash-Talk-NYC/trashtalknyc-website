# Spectator vs Participant Language

Observation:

Trash Talk should avoid making users feel like observers of an organization. There is a tacit assignment of spectator when the first-time viewer sees, "We're keeping New York clean", because as a viewer, one can think, "THEY got it. Whoever 'we' refers to is responsible for that and I'm untethered." 

Preferred language:

- New Yorkers Take Care of New York
- We're getting cleaner and cleaner

Avoid:

- Guilt-Tripping
- Attend events
- Support us
- Watch us

Impacts:

- Website copy
- Partnerships
- Social media
