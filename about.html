Organizer

Trash Talk is New York City's cleanup club for clean streets, parks, and public spaces. By publicly cleaning the streets of New York and advocating for needed infrastructure, we invite our neighbors to care for their streets.

Each of us are the custodians of our communities, and we must advocate for solutions on our own streets. I will continue leading by example through daily cleaning, political advocacy, and social events designed to create more advocates for clean streets. Let's clean New York - together.

Join the movement by registering as a volunteer, attending our weekly events, or cleaning up your own neighborhood. Let's get out there and clean!
